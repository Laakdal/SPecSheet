Reflection cube textures: layout and per-face colour (ETS2 1.60.1.7)
====================================================================

Command (install opened read-only):
  ETS2_DIR=<game> cargo run --release -p specsheet-archive --example scs -- tex <path>
`tex` prints the image and sample records. Then, per face, the colour of the
smallest mip: for BC formats, the mean of the block's two RGB565 endpoints,
sRGB-encoded and approximate.

Observations:
- All four cubes are 6-face BC1 sRGB (DXGI 72) with the cube flag set. They
  are 512^2 (vehicle_reflection) or 256^2, with a full mip chain.
- Face order (Confirmed for the y axis): in every non-uniform cube, face 2
  is the brightest and bluest (sky) and face 3 the darkest and brownish
  (ground). This matches the stored order +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z with +y up,
  i.e. the D3D/DDS convention and the order wgpu expects. Whether the x/z faces
  are mirrored relative to wgpu is not testable this way (Unknown).
- ui_vehicle_reflection.tobj (named by the garage scene's own materials, by no
  truck material): all six faces are the same flat grey (0.258, 0.246, 0.210),
  i.e. a neutral cube without detail.
- The cubes are dark on average: sRGB means of about 0.3-0.7 per face, so
  linear means of about 0.07-0.45.
