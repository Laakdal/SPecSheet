Use in the app: specsheet-data maps DXGI 67 to TextureFormat::Rgb9e5, which
uploads unchanged as wgpu's Rgb9e5Ufloat (the same bit layout). Nothing the
tuning screen draws today samples these textures; they are needed for
skyboxes and HDR UI images.
