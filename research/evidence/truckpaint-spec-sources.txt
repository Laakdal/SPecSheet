truckpaint shader spec: sources read (clean-room SPEC side, D13)
=================================================================

Purpose: audit trail for docs/app/shader-specs/truckpaint.md and truckpaint-lighting.md.
Lists what was read, how it was extracted and what was counted. No shader text is
reproduced here or in the specs. Extracted files live only in the gitignored
research/extracted/truckpaint/ (rfx/, glsl/, txt_*.txt printable-text dumps).

Install: ETS2 1.60.1.7, D:\Steam Library\steamapps\common\Euro Truck Simulator 2 (read-only).

Commands (Git Bash)
-------------------
  export ETS2_DIR="D:/Steam Library/steamapps/common/Euro Truck Simulator 2" MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1
  export CARGO_TARGET_DIR=<scratchpad>/target
  cargo build --release -j 4 -p specsheet-archive --example scs
  cargo build --release -j 4 -p specsheet-data --example model_info
  SCS=$CARGO_TARGET_DIR/release/examples/scs.exe

  # 1. variant list (270 .rfx; 135 skinned, 132 shadow, 132 asafew.a variants not read)
  $SCS ls effect/eut2/truckpaint
  # 2. effect files read
  $SCS cat /effect/eut2/truckpaint/<name>.rfx > research/extracted/truckpaint/rfx/<name>.rfx
  # 3. GLSL objects named by the glsl technique(s) of those effects
  awk '/path: glsl/{g=1} /path: sm4x/{g=0} g && /_setup/ && /_shd/{print $2}' rfx/*.rfx | sort -u
  $SCS cat /effect/eut2/_shd/<hash>.glsl.<vso|fso> > research/extracted/truckpaint/glsl/<hash>...
  # 4. printable text of each object (SHDO container, GLSL 330 inside)
  tr -c '\11\12\15\40-\176' '\n' < glsl/<obj> | grep -a -E '.{3,}' > txt_<obj>.txt
  # 5. truckpaint .mat examples (inputs only)
  model_info /vehicle/truck/volvo_fh_2024/cabin/globe_xl_aero_2024.pmd   -> /automat/a1/a13f598c610360b9.mat (tsnmapuv)
  model_info /vehicle/truck/upgrade/doorhandle/scania_rcab_2009/doorhandle_02.pmd -> /automat/1b/1b3ea38cb5609b77.mat (flipflake.altuv)
  model_info /vehicle/truck/upgrade/doorhandle/scania_streamline/doorhandle_02.pmd -> /automat/6c/6c5cc0ceb449fd28.mat (plain, with paintjob_* keys)
  $SCS cat /automat/14/14a950810c14dfc2.mat   (Scania S cab, plain truckpaint)

Counts and checks (on the extracted text; reproducible with grep over txt_*.fso.txt)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Reads of the "environment" row (5th vec4) of the second material block: 0 of 26
  fragment objects. Fresnel row: only its 3rd component is read, in 26 of 26.
- The packed material-block diffuse (1st row, rgb) is read by 6 of 26 fragment objects:
  exactly the defattr+fwdopaque pairs of the three variants without paint uniforms
  ((none), tsnmap, tsnmapuv).
- Deferred-attribute pass vs forward pass, surface maths up to the point each writes
  its output, after renaming temporaries: identical for 6 of 6 pairs compared
  (base, colormask, airbrush, tsnmap, flipflake, airbrush.flipflake); the forward
  pass additionally computes an output alpha.
- UV routing (vertex objects, 14 of 14 read): UV0 for texture_base always; second
  coordinate pair = mesh tex_coord_1 without altuv, tex_coord_2 with altuv (7 of 7
  paint/flake vertex objects); flipflakeuv adds tex_coord_2 as a third pair (3 of 3);
  tsnmapuv reads a separate tex_coord_6 attribute (3 of 3); tsnmap uses UV0.
- Literal constants observed: 255/127 and 128/127 (normal decode), 0.399902 and 0.600098
  (normal-z diffuse factor), 0.996 (flake clearcoat bias), e = 2.718282, 1/90000 and 20
  (envmap widening), Schlick exponent 5, N.L gate factor 10, 1e-6 (eye-vector clamp),
  2.0 (vertex colour expansion), G-buffer packing 5/6/5 bits + exponent offset 16,
  reflection strength x4096 clamped at 16383.
