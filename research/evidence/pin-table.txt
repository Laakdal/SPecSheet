Pin table of the truck configuration screen (F5)
================================================

Source: /def/truck_config_scene.sii in the mounted 1.60.1.7 install (def.scs),
the config_path of the tuning screen's scene widget. Read with:
  ETS2_DIR=<game dir> MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 cargo run --release -p specsheet-archive     --example scs -- cat /def/truck_config_scene.sii
and tabulated by hand. The parallel arrays pin_group / pin_look / pin_offset /
pin_dir / pin_dir_uk / double_sided have 65 entries. Values below are our
tabulation of those numbers. The app parses the same arrays into
specsheet_data::scene::TuningScene::pins.

Axes are truck space: x right (negative = left side), y up, z backward
(negative = forward).

Per axle, relative to the first entry's point:
  tire  +0.40 up        disc  +0.25 up        rim, hub  at the centre

Every wheel pin faces -x (left) in both directions and is double_sided.

B. Wheel slots without a pin entry
----------------------------------
The truck catalog has 7 wheel slots per axle (specsheet-data catalog.rs
WHEEL_GROUPS): tire, disc, rim, hub, nuts, cover, wheel. f_wheel and r_wheel
(complete wheels) have no pin_group entry. The app's single front-wheel
marker showed the count 7 (screenshot 2026-10-28, Scania S), i.e. all seven
slots stacked on one point.

pin_dir_uk differs from pin_dir only for: cab_door, codrv_seat, s_mirror (x flipped:
the driver's side). pin_look counts: {'custom_acc': 59, 'acc_hub': 2, 'acc_cover': 2, 'acc_nuts': 2}.
Offsets of double_sided entries outside the wheels are mostly x -0.8 to -1.35:
about half a truck's width, so their base point is not the slot's own side
locator (a fender locator at x about -1.2 plus -1.3 would land 2.5 m out).
Pattern: every entry with double_sided false has x offset 0, and every
double_sided entry outside the wheels has x between -0.8 and -1.35, except
cabdefl and r_deflector (offset 0). The double_sided slots are left/right
pairs (fenders, lights, steps, mirrors, side skirts); the single-sided ones sit
on one side or the centre line (exhausts, horns, flags, beacon, rear bumper).
Hypothesis: a double_sided pin starts from the centre of the slot's left and
right locators (x about 0) and the x offset puts it on the truck's side,
mirrored to the side facing the camera. The wheel pins do not fit this (x
-0.1 from the centre line would sit inside the truck), which matches D: they
start from a wheel locator. REA on the pin reader decides both.

D. Observation in the game (the user, 2026-10-28)
-------------------------------------------------
On the tuning screen the rim pin sits at the wheel's locator on the truck, and
the tyre pin is the tire entry's pin_offset added to that point.
