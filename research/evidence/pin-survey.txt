Pin survey: how far the game-rule pins land from their parts (F5)
=================================================================

Tool: crates/specsheet-data/examples/pin_survey.rs
  ETS2_DIR=<game dir> cargo run --release -p specsheet-data --example pin_survey
Install: ETS2 1.60.1.7, every truck's game preset, left-hand drive.

For every exterior pin (built by specsheet_data::pins from the scene's pin
table, see pin-attributes.txt §8) whose slot has placed parts, the distance
from the pin to the bounding box of those parts (0 = on or inside the box).
Pins of slots with nothing placed are counted but not measured.

Reading
-------
- The rule puts pins on their parts: median 0 m, 90 % within 0.1 m.
- The outliers above 1 m are badge slots (badge, badge_b) and s_panel on
  the Renault T and E-Tech T. Their slot names are prefixes of other slots'
  locator names, and the game's first-prefix-match rule (pin-attributes.txt
  §8) can then pick another slot's locator. That is what the decoded rule
  does; whether the game shows these pins there is to be checked in game.
